Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Click any question below to expand the answer.

Our core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time. We operate a flexible hybrid schedule with 2 recommended in-office collaboration days per week (typically Tuesday and Thursday). Check with your direct manager to agree on the schedule that works best for your team.
Salaries are disbursed on the 25th of each month via direct bank transfer. If the 25th falls on a weekend or public holiday, payment is processed on the closest preceding business day. Make sure you submit your bank account details via the HR portal by the 15th to prevent any processing delays.
All full-time employees start with 25 days of annual paid leave, plus local bank holidays. You can submit leave requests through the internal Leave Form link above. Requests are routed to your direct manager for approval within 48 hours.
In addition to your primary laptop, every new team member receives an external 4K monitor, ergonomic keyboard, mouse, and noise-canceling headset. We also provide a one-time $500 home office ergonomics stipend claimable in the Expenses portal during your first 90 days.
The Level 1 canteen serves hot breakfast from 8:00 AM to 10:00 AM and lunch from 11:30 AM to 2:00 PM. Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and halal options are clearly labeled daily. You can preview the full week's menu and chef specials using the Canteen Menu button in the Useful Links section.
